The contract that lets two people work at once

Agree this before anyone writes code.

Split the system at the action name. Track A turns camera pixels into a string. Track B turns that string into something happening on the machine. Neither needs the other to be finished.

Shared vocabulary — fix these strings now, never rename them

mute_toggle media_playpause privacy_blank privacy_restore playlist_open led_beat_start led_beat_stop break_prompt

How B works without a camera

python -m handsfree.actions --fire mute_toggle — fires any action straight from the terminal. B builds and tests every action this way while A is still fighting the gesture recogniser.

File ownership — no branches, you don't have time to merge

A owns sources.py, gestures.py, cursor.py. B owns actions/, beats.py. Commit to master every 20 minutes. If you need a file the other owns, ask — don't edit.

Bench cards

Build it, then run the test, then check the number. A feature without a passing bench test is not built.

Track A

Kill switch & safety rails

20 min · do this first
Build

pynput global hotkey Ctrl+Alt+Q → os._exit(0). pyautogui.FAILSAFE = True. Cursor control armed only while a pose is held — drop your hand and control stops.

Test
  1. Run a script that walks the cursor in a 200 px circle forever.
  2. While it is moving, press Ctrl+Alt+Q.
  3. Repeat 3×, including once while a modal dialog holds focus.
  4. Separately, slam the cursor into a screen corner — failsafe must abort.
PASS — cursor stops and process exits ≤ 200 ms, 3 of 3, including with focus stolen.
Stop everything if this fails. Once the app owns the mouse and a gesture misfires, you cannot click "stop" — you have given away the only input device you had.
Track A

Gesture recognition

45 min
Build

MediaPipe GestureRecognizer gives Thumb_Up, Open_Palm, Victory, Closed_Fist free — no training. Finger-to-lips needs geometry: index tip (landmark 8) within a radius of the mouth landmark from the face mesh.

Test
  1. Hold each gesture 10× at 0.5 m, then 1.0 m, then 1.5 m from the camera.
  2. Log every detection with its confidence and timestamp.
  3. Then sit for 60 s talking and moving your hands naturally, making no intentional gestures. Count what fires.
PASS — ≥ 8/10 detected within 1 s at all three distances, and ≤ 1 false positive in 60 s of normal talking.

The false-positive number is the one that decides whether the demo survives. Most teams never measure it, then their screen blanks mid-sentence on stage.

Track A

Cursor, pinch click, scroll

75 min · the headline claim
Build

Index tip → active region 0.25–0.75 of the frame mapped to the full screen. One Euro filter, not plain LERP. Click when dist(4,8) / dist(0,9) < 0.35 — a ratio, so it survives you leaning toward the camera. Scroll on two-finger vertical drag.

Test
  1. Jitter — hold your hand still for 10 s, log cursor position.
  2. Targeting — five 60 px buttons at the four corners and centre. Time each acquisition.
  3. Click — pinch on a 60 px target 10×.
  4. Depth — repeat the click test leaning in, then leaning back.
  5. Scroll — a long page, three screens down and back.
PASS — jitter std-dev < 5 px · 5/5 targets acquired ≤ 5 s each with no oscillation · ≥ 8/10 clicks land, no double-fire · same hit rate leaning in and out · scroll stops when the hand stops.

Step 4 is the bug in Ankit's version — his fixed 0.045 threshold makes pinching easier as you lean in. If your ratio version passes and his doesn't, that is a slide-free thing to show.

Track B

Mute & pause — the desk-visitor moment

40 min
Prerequisite that will bite you. Zoom only responds to Alt+A from another window if you tick Enable Global Shortcut under Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts → Mute/Unmute My Audio. Do this now, not at 19:00.
Build

System mute via VK_VOLUME_MUTE, media via VK_MEDIA_PLAY_PAUSE, Zoom via global Alt+A.

Test
  1. Play music. --fire media_playpause. Sound stops.
  2. Time gesture → silence.
  3. Join a test meeting from your phone. Fire mute_toggle with Zoom not focused. Watch the mute indicator on the phone.
  4. Fire again — audio returns.
PASS — latency < 500 ms · 5/5 in both directions · works while Zoom is unfocused.
Track B

Stealth privacy blank

25 min · cheapest win here
Build

A Tk Toplevel, -fullscreen, -topmost, black fill. Bind Esc to dismiss.

Test
  1. --fire privacy_blank — screen covers in under a second.
  2. Press Esc. It must dismiss from the keyboard.
  3. With an external display attached, check what happens. Decide now: cover every monitor, or only the primary.
  4. While blanked, confirm Ctrl+Alt+Q still works.
PASS — appears ≤ 1 s · Esc dismisses · kill switch still reachable underneath.
Step 2 is not optional. A topmost black window with no keyboard escape locks you out of your own laptop in front of the room.
Track B

Thumbs-up → playlist + LED on the beat

50 min · needs the Arduino
Build

Playlist via os.startfile() on a local file or a Spotify URI. For the LED: beat-track the song offline with librosa beat_track, then replay timed pulses over serial.

Do not attempt realtime loopback audio. WASAPI loopback capture on Windows is fiddly and you have three hours. Offline beat times are deterministic, which also means they can't fail on stage.
Test
  1. --fire playlist_open — player opens and plays.
  2. Print detected BPM. Tap along to sanity-check it.
  3. Film 20 s of the LED on a phone with the music playing. Watch it back.
  4. Unplug the board. Confirm the on-screen pulse fallback still runs.
PASS — no visible drift over 20 s · degrades to the screen pulse when no board is connected.
Track B

Movement → break reminder

25 min · build last, cut without regret
Build

MediaPipe Pose. Track the shoulder–hip midpoint, accumulate movement energy over a rolling window, prompt when it stays under threshold.

Test
  1. Set the window to 30 s for the demo. The real value is 20 minutes and you cannot demo 20 minutes.
  2. Sit still 30 s — prompt fires.
  3. Stand and stretch — timer resets.
PASS — fires at 30 s of stillness · resets within 3 s of real movement.

Integration test — 17:45, both of you

This is the one that decides whether you have a demo.

Webcam contention with Zoom

  1. Open Zoom. Turn your video on.
  2. Start the app. Does it bind the camera?
  3. If not: turn Zoom video off, retry.
  4. Still failing: route through OBS virtual camera, or pitch from a second device with the laptop as the demo machine.
Zoom wants the camera for your pitch. So does your app. This is the failure that leaves you with nothing at 20:00, and it takes two minutes to rule out.

What the room actually sees

  1. Share your screen on Zoom. Join the same meeting from your phone.
  2. Look at the phone. Is the HUD text legible at that size?
  3. Check the venue lighting — MediaPipe degrades badly in backlight. Retune thresholds where you'll actually stand.
  4. Run the full 90-second sequence three times consecutively.
DONE means three clean consecutive runs. Not one lucky one.
